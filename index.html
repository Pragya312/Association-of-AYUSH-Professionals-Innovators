About The Organization

Building a Healthier & Skilled India through AYUSH

Established on 2nd February 2016, The Association of AYUSH Professionals and Innovators is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing healthcare and education. By integrating traditional wellness systems with modern learning methodologies, the Association strives to foster a healthier, skilled, and self-reliant India in alignment with the national vision of Viksit Bharat@2047.

The Association views innovative education and quality healthcare as the twin drivers of sustainable social progress. Education empowers individuals by broadening knowledge and career opportunities, while strategic healthcare initiatives ensure the physical and mental well-being required for a productive society.

About the Association

Integrating Traditional Wisdom with Modern Evidence

Grounded in scientific, evidence-based frameworks, our programs cultivate resilient communities equipped to meet future challenges.

Core Vision

"To build healthier, educated, nutritionally secure and empowered communities through an integrated approach that combines AYUSH principles, preventive healthcare, and nutrition, education and community development."

We envision a society where every individual has access to knowledge, essential health support and opportunities for a dignified and sustainable life.

Core Mission

"To serve communities through integrated AYUSH, healthcare, nutrition, education and awareness initiatives, with a special focus on vulnerable populations and the prevention of malnutrition."

Through evidence-informed AYUSH practices, community participation, and targeted social interventions, we create measurable and lasting social impact.

Holistic Strategy

Key Pillars of Intervention

Seven strategic pillars designed to address root causes of social and healthcare vulnerability across India.

AYUSH & Preventive Healthcare

Promoting holistic wellness, preventive practices, traditional health systems, and proactive community health awareness.

Nutrition & Malnutrition

Supporting nutrition awareness, regular screening, supplementation, and community interventions like Poshan Kits.

Education & Awareness

Promoting health, nutrition, hygiene and life-skills education among children, families, and rural school networks.

Women & Child Welfare

Supporting the overall health, nutritional security, education, and development of mothers, adolescent girls, and children.

Community Empowerment

Encouraging grassroots participation, localized capacity building, and sustainable solutions to unique social challenges.

Healthy & Sustainable Environment

Promoting environmentally responsible, resilient practices that contribute to long-term public health and clean living.

Evidence-Based Action

Utilizing rigorous scientific knowledge, field-level assessments, continuous monitoring, and measurable outcomes to maximize the impact of every intervention.
